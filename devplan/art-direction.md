# Art Direction

## Research Summary

Sources reviewed: official homepage, official about page, official videos page, official press kit, and Bandcamp profile.

Public-facing visual and thematic anchors:
- The project is anonymous; do not speculate about real identities.
- The duo are presented as incongruous beings with oversized papier-mache heads.
- The language around the act is anti-arena, mantra-rock, pythago-cubist, cardboard/cartonneux, humorous, and excessive.
- Khn is tied to microtonal electric guitar/bass layers, angular quarter-tone phrases, and looped ornaments.
- Klek is tied to percussion, controlled asymmetrical grooves, and dynamic addition/removal of patterns.
- Press-kit mythology references space-time voyagers fascinated by hot dogs, pyramids, and rock grandeur.

## Direction

Create original sprites that feel like a low-budget alien stage ritual rather than generic punk, anime, or glossy mascot art. Use huge lumpy papier-mache mask heads, stark body shapes, cardboard theater props, blunt silhouettes, absurd confidence, and rhythmic geometric motifs.

## Protagonist Rules

- Khn and Klek should have oversized cream/cardboard mask heads with imperfect handmade outlines.
- Their bodies should be black, stark, and stage-costume-like.
- Sparse white dots can remain on protagonist outfits because the project brief requests it, but dots are not a universal world rule.
- Khn reads through an extended microtonal guitar silhouette, angular slash arcs, quarter-tone wave effects, and agile leaning poses.
- Klek reads through sticks, cymbal circles, drum shockwaves, stomp poses, and heavier squared timing.

## Enemy Rules

- Enemies do not need polka dots and should generally avoid them.
- Enemies should read as music-industry archetypes first: suit blocks, badges, lanyards, phones, clipboards, contracts, vinyl crates, festival maps, vampire capes, influencer ring lights.
- Their color palette can diverge from the protagonists: corporate blue, sickly green, dull gray, warning yellow, contract beige, venue-stamp red.
- Keep enemies fictional and archetypal, not depictions of real people.

## Prompt Template

Original side-scrolling beat em up sprite, anonymous surreal anti-arena rock performer, oversized lumpy papier mache mask head, black theatrical costume with sparse white dot accents only on protagonist, cardboard stage ritual aesthetic, angular microtonal energy, thick readable silhouette, limited palette, no real person likeness, no copied costume, no logo.

## Immediate Procedural Placeholder Pass

- Protagonist dots only: keep sparse white dots on Khn/Klek costumes, never as a default enemy motif.
- Oversized papier-mache heads: protagonists should have lumpy cream/cardboard mask heads readable at gameplay scale.
- Khn model-sheet target: slim black stage body, forward lean, long exaggerated microtonal guitar/bass neck, irregular fret ticks, angular white string trails.
- Klek model-sheet target: stockier planted body, sticks, compact floor drum, hanging cymbal/plate, radial percussion shockwaves, squarer lumpy mask.
- Second-pass Khn target: pinched/beaked papier-mache mask, double-neck hybrid instrument, cable belt/loop module, splayed toe controls, nervous precision.
- Second-pass Klek target: broad pyramid-boulder papier-mache mask, sherpa straps, asymmetric matte percussion rig, mallet/stick contrast, overloaded grounded posture.
- Enemy prop silhouettes: use phones, clipboards, cables, ring lights, boxes, records, megaphones, and corporate badges to define archetypes.
- Cardboard absurdity: stage backgrounds should include low-contrast cardboard pyramids, hot-dog signage, curtains, and rough stage flats.
- UI readability: dark backing plates are required behind HUD and overlay text.

# Level Design

## Milestone 1 Stage

Basement Rehearsal Catacombs is represented as a cardboard anti-arena rehearsal floor with curtains, wood lanes, and a low-budget stage-card feel.

## Future Stage Anchors

- Basement Rehearsal Catacombs: cables, amps, cardboard pyramids, hot dog wrappers, looping pedal shrine.
- Festival Backstage Labyrinth: wristbands, barricades, confusing signage, branded tents.
- Streaming Tower: vertical UI glass, algorithm screens, playlist elevators.
- The Anti-Arena: collapsing theatrical grandeur, fake pyrotechnics, oversized stage props.

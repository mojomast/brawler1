# Combat Spec

Milestone 1 implements Khn only.

## Khn

- Movement: side-scroll plus shallow lane movement.
- Jump: hop for evasion and game feel.
- Dash: short horizontal burst.
- Light: fast jagged guitar slash, builds meter.
- Heavy: slower guitar strike, higher knockback.
- Special: forward dissonant wave, costs full meter.

## Values

Values live in `src/game/config/constants.js` and should remain tunable there.

## Acceptance

- Attacks damage enemies once per activation.
- Enemy hit reactions are visible.
- Player can take damage with invulnerability flicker.
- Special meter fills through hits and kills.

# Long-Nose Dot-Suit Persona Review

## What was built

Updated procedural protagonist art to more strongly echo Angine de Poitrine's public stage persona: oversized lumpy papier-mache masks, very long noses, black-and-white dot-suit language, Khn's double-neck microtonal instrument, Klek's compact percussion kit, and triangle ritual motifs.

## What works

- Both protagonists now have much clearer long-nose mask silhouettes at gameplay scale.
- Dot patterns are protagonist-forward and cover the body as bold bands rather than sparse isolated spots.
- Khn reads more strongly as the agile microtonal guitar/bass performer through double necks, dense frets, loop module, and dotted feet.
- Klek reads more strongly as the grounded percussion performer through a compact busy kit, mallet/stick contrast, drum/cymbal forms, and triangular shock glyphs.
- Enemies remain music-industry archetypes rather than polka-dot masked performers.

## Compromises

- Dots are grouped into large readable bands instead of being all-over tiny speckling to avoid noise at gameplay scale.
- Mask/nose shapes are original abstractions and not matched to any specific public photo.

## Next recommendation

Use this procedural pass as a model sheet for a future sprite-sheet pass with authored idle/run/attack frames.

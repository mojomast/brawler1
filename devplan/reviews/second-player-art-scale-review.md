# Second Player Art Scale Review

## What was built

Focused second art pass on `ProtagonistArt.js`: stronger mask/nose silhouettes, controlled full-body dot patterns, clearer feet/boots, state-aware procedural animation, hidden debug hitboxes, and triangle special glyphs.

## What works

- Khn reads more agile and nervy through stronger run lean, smaller denser dot bands, dotted feet, and longer microtonal neck motion.
- Klek reads more grounded through chunkier boots, larger structured dot rows, heavier mask bob, and percussion shock glyphs.
- Debug attack rectangles no longer appear during normal play.
- Triangle ritual motifs now appear on specials without adding new mechanics.

## Compromises

- Dot coverage remains grouped into bands rather than every surface being filled, to avoid noise at gameplay scale.
- Procedural masks remain original abstractions rather than exact public-photo copies.

## Next recommendation

Use screenshots from actual play to tune dot size and mask nose length before any sprite-sheet pass.

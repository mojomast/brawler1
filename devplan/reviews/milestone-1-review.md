# Milestone 1 Review

## What was built

Dependency-free browser Canvas vertical slice with title, Khn movement, jump, dash, light/heavy/special attacks, one generic enemy AI, wave spawning, HUD, pause, game over, win, and restart.

## What works

Core loop is playable on paper: enter game, fight waves, fill meter, defeat enemies, and reach completion.

## What feels weak

Visuals are still procedural placeholders. Hit stop, sound, camera shake, stronger enemy tells, and richer stage props are missing.

## Technical debt created

Canvas drawing methods are embedded in entity classes. This is acceptable for Milestone 1 but should be separated if art complexity grows.

## Research/art decision summary

Updated art direction after official-source research. Protagonists should emphasize oversized papier-mache heads, black theatrical costume, and requested sparse white dots. Enemies should not generally use polka dots and should instead read through satirical industry props and silhouettes.

## Next milestone recommendation

Run QA, tune feel, then proceed to Milestone 2: Klek and differentiated move identity.

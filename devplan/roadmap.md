# Roadmap

## Milestone 0: Planning And Scaffolding

Status: complete enough for Milestone 1.

Deliverables:
- Static browser app with modular Canvas game loop.
- Devplan structure and subagent workflow assumptions.
- Vertical slice acceptance criteria.
- Research-aligned art direction.

## Milestone 1: Vertical Slice

Status: implemented, under first review.

Acceptance criteria:
- Title screen starts the game.
- Khn can move left/right, change lanes, jump, dash, light attack, heavy attack, and special attack.
- One basic enemy archetype can chase, attack, take hitstun, and be defeated.
- Three simple waves spawn and advance.
- HUD shows health, special, wave, and enemy count.
- Pause, game over, win, and restart loops work.
- Placeholder art reflects Angine de Poitrine research without copying exact copyrighted imagery.

## Milestone 2: Dual Protagonists

Status: complete for prototype.

Deliverables:
- Khn and Klek selectable from title/select flow.
- Khn uses jagged guitar attacks and dissonant forward special.
- Klek uses heavier percussion identity, cymbal/stick silhouettes, larger shockwave special, more health, and slower movement.

## Milestone 3: Enemy Roster Expansion

Status: complete for prototype.

Deliverables:
- Eight satirical non-polka-dot enemy archetype labels and stat variants.
- WaveDirector sequences varied enemy compositions.
- Enemy art uses corporate/music-industry props instead of protagonist costume identity.

## Milestone 4: Boss Verticals

Status: complete for prototype.

Deliverables:
- Arena Promoter.
- Content Mill Hydra.
- Grand Executive of Palatability.
- Boss health, phase changes, speed/damage escalation, and add summons.

## Milestone 5: Stage Expansion

Status: complete for prototype.

Deliverables:
- Basement Rehearsal Catacombs.
- Festival Backstage Labyrinth.
- Streaming Tower.
- The Anti-Arena.
- Stage cards and stage-specific wave sequencing.

## Milestone 6: Art Pass And Cohesion

Status: P1/P2 procedural placeholder complete.

Deliverables:
- Oversized papier-mache protagonist head silhouettes.
- Protagonist-only sparse dot costume motif.
- Non-polka-dot enemy silhouettes.
- Stage-card and HUD readability pass.

## Milestone 7: QA, Balancing, Packaging

Status: local static QA complete; manual browser playtest remains recommended.

Deliverables:
- `README.md` run and control documentation.
- `npm run build`, `npm run test`, and module syntax checks passing.
- Final review and handoff artifacts.

## Do Not Jump Ahead

Future work should avoid adding new scope until manual playtest tuning is complete. Prioritize feel, readable tells, hit stop, audio, and replacing procedural art with sprite sheets.
